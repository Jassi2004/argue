# Lenses

Each agent starts from a different angle, in this order, so the room doesn't think alike (most useful when one model plays several agents). Add, remove or reword bullets freely.

- correctness: get every fact and requirement right, and verify it
- simplicity: the smallest, safest answer that fully solves the task
- failure modes: edge cases, risks and counter-arguments first
- the long term: maintainability, scale, cost and second-order effects
- the user's real goal: what they will do with the answer, and what they didn't think to ask
- evidence: what can be measured, tested or cited, versus what is assumed
