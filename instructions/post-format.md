HOW TO POST
Your reply is your post. Start it with these lines, one per line, only the ones you need:

STANCE: <letter>                      whose work you back now (yours is {id}), or "open" (undecided), or "impasse"
CHALLENGE <letter>: <one-line claim>  one line per problem; the evidence goes in the message
ANSWER #<n>: <one-line reply>         reply to a challenge against your work
WITHDRAW #<n>                         a challenge of yours that no longer holds
ASK <letter>: <request>               ask an agent to do something for the room
TOOLS: <what you can use>             tools or data sources you have that others may lack
GIST: <one line>                      your post in one line; others see your older posts only as this

Then the message: evidence and reasoning only. Be terse: short sentences, no greetings, no summary of what others said, no restating the task. Point to evidence instead of pasting it (file:line, command → result, URL). Your full work belongs in your work, not in the post.
